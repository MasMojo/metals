Safety checklist

This is practical hygiene for a private metal sale, not legal advice. Rules and insurance limits change — verify before you ship or pay.

  • Verify reputation and feedback before you send money or metal. Read a user’s history, not just a single post.
  • On r/Pmsforsale, comment on the listing before dealing — that is a subreddit rule, not optional courtesy.
  • Beware of impersonators and typo usernames. Confirm you are talking to the account that made the post.
  • New members typically ship first, or both sides use a trusted middleman, until a track record exists.
  • Use Registered Mail for bullion. Regular USPS indemnity for coins and bullion is commonly only $15; Registered Mail can insure up to $50,000. Confirm current USPS rules and declared value before you ship.
  • Avoid PayPal Friends & Family — there is no purchase protection. PayPal also excludes most precious-metal bullion from Purchase Protection, so a Goods & Services payment may still not cover gold or silver. Use a method both sides understand.
  • For local cash deals, meet in public (a well-used place, ideally with cameras). Do not go to a private home or parking garage with a stranger and a stack of metal or cash.
  • Test the metal: a magnet check, ping, scale and calipers, and a Sigma or XRF if you have access. Fake premium coins exist.
  • Keep records for taxes: what you paid, what you sold for, dates, and shipping. Reporting rules depend on your situation; this page does not prepare a return.

Where to trade

Public marketplaces and forums, listed only as places people use. This is not an endorsement, and none of them are affiliated with Stack Mojo. Read each venue’s rules and fee schedule yourself. No personal locations are listed here — look up coin shows and clubs in your own area.

  • r/Pmsforsale rules — peer listings on Reddit. Start with the rules; fees are typically just shipping.
  • Pure (collectpure.com) — peer marketplace with an approximate ~1% fee (verify).
  • PeerMetals — peer marketplace; illustrative fee used here is 2.75% (verify).
  • Whatnot — live auctions; blended fees are often around ~7% and vary (verify).
  • eBay — huge audience, higher fees (illustrative 13.6%; often also charged on shipping — verify the current schedule).
  • Local coin shows and clubs — in-person inspection, usually cash or a mutually agreed payment. Search ANA, state numismatic associations, or a local club calendar. This site does not recommend a venue or meeting place.
