Price history

1W through All, using public history committed to this project. Metals series are from Standard Bullion (with required attribution). Bitcoin and ether are from CoinGecko. Copper uses FRED monthly global prices (converted to $/lb) plus daily snapshots. The performance index can overlay CPI-U and optionally show real, inflation-adjusted returns.

The performance index rebases each series to 100 at the start of the selected window so you can compare percentage moves, not dollar prices. In nominal mode a dashed CPI-U line is overlaid, also rebased to 100. Turn on Real (inflation-adjusted) to deflate prices with that CPI (latest month = 100 cents of purchasing power) and then rebase. CPI is monthly and lags — the note above names the latest observation in the committed file.

Indicative prices, not quotes, not financial advice. Figures can be delayed or wrong. Verify before you trade.

Live last prices: Gold API. Bid/ask, day change, and metals history: Data by Standard Bullion. Bitcoin and ether 24-hour change and last-year history: CoinGecko. Longer Bitcoin history: Blockchain.com. CPI-U and monthly copper: FRED series CPIAUCSL and PCOPPUSDM (no API key; copper converted from USD/metric ton to USD/lb).
